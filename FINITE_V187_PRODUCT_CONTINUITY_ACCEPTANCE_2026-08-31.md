# Finite v187 product continuity acceptance — 2026-08-31

## Scope

Finite v187 repairs a live cross-route continuity defect and makes the existing reality-change loop read as one calm product experience. It does not add a competition mode, a new planning family, or an engineering-first explanation layer. The validated source baseline was `efa282be0432ad4ac2a2a5eed0143878bbad2201`.

## Repairs accepted

- A waiting arrival no longer directs Codex into the capabilities of an unrelated accepted plan. Finite now opens the bounded construction toolset and carries an exact follow-up blueprint action derived from the current rough plan's family, order version, and checksum.
- Arrival family selection works before a complete editable starter plan exists, while interview and recurring-practice briefs retain their intentionally general composable family.
- An adversarial accepted-travel / waiting-dinner journey proves the directed action opens the construction route and returns an event blueprint rather than the accepted travel plan.
- The decision surface now explains what changed, what must stay true, and what the person is choosing before presenting options.
- Option and approval language is consumer-facing. It says what each choice changes, what remains, and the boundary of the action without leading with revisions or protocol language.
- The latest applied option now leaves a persistent consequence card showing the forecast movement, remaining buffer, protected constraints, and the concrete compromise that landed.
