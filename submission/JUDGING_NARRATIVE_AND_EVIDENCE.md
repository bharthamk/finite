# Finite judging narrative and evidence

Use this as the claim-control layer for the Devpost text, video, screenshots,
README, and live demo. Do not add a stronger claim unless it has a source below.

## One-sentence thesis

Finite makes WebMCP consequential without making it reckless: Codex can work a
real change against canonical plan state, while the person alone decides what
becomes accepted truth.

## Stage-one viability

- **Theme fit:** the website becomes materially more capable when a person and
  their agent work in the same visible plan.
- **Non-trivial WebMCP:** seven imperative document tools provide canonical
  entry, bounded semantic routing, exact detail recovery, staged decisions,
  authority-bound apply, receipts, and guided view.
- **Working product:** public v241 runs without judge credentials through the
  synthetic Spotlight and preserves revision 2 across reload.
- **Public proof:** source, build instructions, live URL, video, and license are
  all required before submission; only repository/video/license owner actions
  remain open.

## 1. WebMCP leverage

**Judge takeaway:** This workflow is better because the page can offer semantic,
revision-aware capabilities directly to the agent while keeping the person on
the same visible surface.

Proof to show:

- `document.modelContext.registerTool(...)` registrations in
  `src/webmcp-bootstrap.ts` and dispatch in `src/webmcp.ts`.
- Seven stable document tools instead of route-by-route registry growth.
- Three calls to first useful action and zero failed calls in the accepted run.
- Exact candidate, revision, evidence, and five-minute human authority checks.
- Idempotent replay and content-addressed exact-detail recovery.

## 2. Execution

**Judge takeaway:** Finite is a complete planning product, not a protocol demo.

Proof to show:

- A public responsive application spanning plan creation, editing, activation,
  management, replanning, evidence, collaboration, completion, and recovery.
- 358/358 tests, 20 hostile Spotlight kernel runs, production builds, chunk budget,
  keyboard operation, mobile target floor, and reload continuity.
- Cloudflare D1 accepted truth, R2 attachments, optimistic concurrency, atomic
  receipts, isolated browser-local Demo mode, and account-scoped persistence.
- Production receipt and visible before/after revision.

## 3. Potential impact

**Judge takeaway:** People already plan across chats, spreadsheets, and task
lists; the expensive failure is what happens when a real assumption changes.

Finite reduces the cost of replanning while preserving accountability. It is
useful anywhere dates, money, commitments, evidence, and trade-offs must change
together: travel, renovation, events, interview preparation, and recurring
practice are already represented by one adaptive grammar.

Do not claim measured market adoption or time savings. No fresh external user
cohort exists yet.

## 4. Creativity and ambition

**Judge takeaway:** Finite treats an agent interaction as a versioned decision
transaction rather than a chat response or autonomous click sequence.

The distinctive design is the separation of pressure, legal option search,
human choice, exact authority, accepted revision, and real-world execution
state. Research, quote, hold, book, pay, verify, and cancel remain different
truth states. The architecture generalizes beyond travel without introducing a
backend model or an application-owned agent.
