# Finite demo video script

Target: 2:30–2:40. Hard maximum: 2:59. Use the v241 public synthetic Spotlight.

## 0:00–0:12 — The problem

**Picture:** Active Europe plan. Hold on fixed flights, total budget, and AUD
freedom.

**Narration:**

“Plans rarely fail all at once. One cost moves, a date changes, or a commitment
becomes fixed—and suddenly every connected choice needs another pass.”

## 0:12–0:27 — Why WebMCP

**Picture:** Open the Codex handoff and briefly show the page tools.

**Narration:**

“Finite lets Codex work directly with the same visible, versioned plan I’m
looking at. WebMCP gives the agent semantic tools from the page, instead of
making it guess from pixels or asking me to restate the plan in chat.”

## 0:27–0:47 — Record pressure without changing truth

**Picture:** Codex records the synthetic request to add three Paris nights. Keep
revision 1 visible.

**Narration:**

“I want three more nights in Paris, but the international flights stay fixed
and I need at least five hundred Australian dollars of freedom. Finite records
that pressure without pretending the accepted plan has changed.”

## 0:47–1:14 — Bounded search

**Picture:** Show 26 checked, 18 legal, 8 rejected, and the three route cards.

**Narration:**

“Its deterministic kernel checks twenty-six bounded combinations. Eighteen are
legal, eight are rejected, and three distinct routes reach me. Each says what it
protects, what it trades, and why it fits the current plan.”

## 1:14–1:40 — Human authority

**Picture:** Select **Protect breathing room**, open its approval, and pause on
the exact effect before confirming.

**Narration:**

“Codex can do the work, but it cannot choose for me. I select Protect breathing
room, inspect the exact effect, and separately confirm this one revision. The
approval is bound to this candidate, this plan revision, and a short expiry.”

## 1:40–2:02 — Apply exactly once

**Picture:** Codex re-enters, sees authority, applies, and opens the receipt.

**Narration:**

“Codex re-enters canonical state and applies only those returned identifiers.
The plan advances once—from revision one to revision two. Trip and booked
duration move from eighteen to twenty-one days, while nine hundred and ten
dollars of freedom remains.”

## 2:02–2:23 — Proof and durability

**Picture:** Hold on before/after receipt, then reload and show revision 2 and
the same receipt.

**Narration:**

“The receipt keeps the before, after, authority, search, and replay proof
together. Reloading keeps the accepted revision and the same receipt. Repeating
the apply cannot create another change.”

## 2:23–2:38 — Close

**Picture:** Finite mark, product surface, and URL.

**Narration:**

“Finite turns replanning into a visible transaction between a person, their
agent, and accepted truth. Codex works the change. You choose what becomes
true.”

## Recording notes

- Read naturally; do not accelerate beyond comfortable comprehension.
- If the edit exceeds 2:45, shorten pauses before removing explanatory lines.
- Keep one pointer highlight per consequential human action.
- Use captions matching the final spoken audio.
- No music is needed.
