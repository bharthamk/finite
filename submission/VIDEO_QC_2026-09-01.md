fixed menu of three choices. The opening establishes the category before the
travel example. The restaurant model explains the relationship between the
person, Codex, Finite, and WebMCP. The Spotlight proof then shows five
meaningfully different directions because five exist for that plan, while the
narration states that a plan with one workable direction would return one.
