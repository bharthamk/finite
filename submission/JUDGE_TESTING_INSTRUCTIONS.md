# Test Finite

No account or credentials are required. The Spotlight uses a prepared example
plan and keeps its work in the current browser.

Accepted release: Sites v245, marker `hosted-release-marker-v245`.

## Open the live route

1. Open [Finite Spotlight](https://finite.bharthamk.chatgpt.site/?start=spotlight-active&tour=spotlight&plan=1&fresh=1)
   in ChatGPT's in-app browser.
2. Wait for **More Paris, without losing the trip** and the visible
   **Demo mode · Local only** badge.
3. Select **Hand off to Codex**, then **Start the live walkthrough**. If Finite
   gives you an introduction to send, paste it into the current Codex task and
   choose the built-in browser when asked where to watch.

The starting plan is already active. It lasts 18 days, its international
flights are fixed, and at least AUD 500 must remain spare. The prepared change
adds three nights in Paris while keeping those conditions intact.

From there, test it naturally. Ask Codex to explain the plan, compare the
workable directions, question the trade-offs or continue refining the change.
Finite brings the results into the visible product. You can inspect the
comparison without updating the plan, or choose any direction that makes sense
to you and review its exact effect before continuing.

## What you can inspect

- **The same plan in both places.** The dates, costs, conditions and current
  revision shown by Codex match the visible Finite plan.
- **A connected change check.** For this prepared change, Finite currently
  checks 26 combinations. Eighteen work, eight do not, and five meaningfully
  different directions remain. Another plan may return one direction or
  several.
- **Visible trade-offs.** Each direction explains what changes and what stays
  intact. You are free to compare, ask more questions or choose a different
  route from the example shown in the film.
- **A durable result.** If you choose and confirm a direction, Codex continues
  from that exact choice. Reload the current tab afterwards to inspect the new
  revision and its change receipt.
- **The broader product.** Use the plan menu to create another plan, start from
  a template or explore the longer guided tour. Finite changes its workspace
  around the work rather than reusing the Europe layout everywhere.

For reference, choosing **Protect breathing room** produces the result used in
the submission: a 21-day trip with the flights fixed and AUD 910 still spare.
Other directions intentionally produce different trade-offs.

## Browser support

ChatGPT's in-app browser is the shortest route because the WebMCP connection is
available there. Keep the Finite tab open while working with Codex.

For Chrome, use version 149 or later, enable
`chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing`, restart Chrome and reopen the Spotlight
link. If the tools do not appear immediately, refresh the current Finite tab
once.

The visible plan remains available for inspection without WebMCP. The submitted
video also shows the complete live change, comparison, choice, update and reload
flow.
