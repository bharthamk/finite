## Route exercised in every journey

1. The human creates an arrival order and adds a constraint.
2. Codex reconciles known, inferred, missing, dependencies, and one exact human boundary.
3. The human answers on the Site and reviews the resulting interpretation.
4. Codex creates an arrival-bound exact intake with a plan-specific recovery menu, then compiles it to a checksum-bound draft.
5. Selected journeys return that draft; human feedback survives and a materially revised draft replaces it.
6. The human confirms the exact draft outside WebMCP; Codex activates it idempotently.
7. Activation closes the originating arrival order so it cannot re-enter construction forever.
8. A human preference reversal is recorded, staged, separately confirmed, and applied.
9. A normal live change is compared, staged, approved, and applied.
10. A provisional late change is superseded by a newer shock; every stale candidate is invalidated.
11. A deliberately impossible 900,000-minor shock returns `NO_VALID_OPTION` instead of manufactured viability.
12. The human chooses a bounded scope fallback; selected journeys also correct an actual or create an immutable amendment.
13. The human explicitly completes, pauses, or abandons the plan; Finite receipts that lifecycle change and blocks accidental further work.
14. Export integrity, reload, revision, lifecycle, receipt lineage, and finite-total conservation are verified.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **Plans had no ending.** Added accepted `active`, `paused`, `completed`, and `abandoned` lifecycle truth with exact human confirmation, idempotent receipts, persistence, reload, reopening, inactive-route orientation, and mutation refusal.
2. **Arrival orders were immortal after activation.** Activation now atomically attempts terminal arrival acceptance against the exact order version/checksum and exposes reconciliation if closure fails. Retry/replay also attempts closure after a lost response.
3. **The safe tool route could strand Codex.** Staged preference, correction, and lifecycle results now advertise the decision tools required for their next exact apply; accepted results return to planning.
4. **Production construction could lose the recovery menu.** Arrival-bound typed intake now accepts at most twelve plan-specific moves plus bounded search policy. The compiler accepts only those supplied moves and never copies example moves from a family blueprint.
5. **Inactive plans still looked operable.** The kitchen packet and chef menu now surface the ending and ask for human reopening rather than offering ordinary change tools.
6. **The consumer had no closure control.** The Site now exposes compact complete, pause, abandon, and reopen controls, with a separate review/confirmation state and a readable receipt.

## Remaining boundary

The deterministic kitchen can preserve truth, generate and compare bounded moves, stage choices, and enforce human authority. It cannot know that a flight was actually booked, a supplier agreed, a guest was contacted, or a person will behave consistently unless evidence or an authorized external integration reports it. Those are deliberately represented as dependencies rather than guessed completion.
