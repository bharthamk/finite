## Product acceptance

v242 keeps the complete Finite journey and closes the ten P1 judge-path defects
found in the final code-and-product review:

1. Explicit fresh, collaboration and guided routes now outrank a remembered
   browser-local demo.
2. Guided tours begin and end in one isolated local workspace without creating
   a contradictory server demo.
3. First-use pages no longer hydrate non-existent built-in accepted plans,
   construction packets or secondary records.
4. A successful WebMCP mutation or idempotent replay remains successful when
   the optional next-action refresh is unavailable.
5. Unsafe integer money and any option that would produce a negative forecast
   fail closed before staging or apply.
6. End-before-start dates are refused by both the human form and presentation
   reducer.
7. Unknown total limits carry their category forecast into a conserved finite
   allocation rather than silently zeroing it.
8. Travel activations seed equal trip and booked duration entities.
9. The Hobart weekend template respects Hobart, two travellers, weekend length
   and domestic travel semantics instead of importing long-haul assumptions.
10. Dialogs support Escape, restore focus and preserve failed text and numeric
    edits across rerender.

The public Spotlight remains the shortest judge proof of the core transaction:
one live revision, bounded alternatives, visible human choice, exact authority,
one accepted apply and a durable receipt.
